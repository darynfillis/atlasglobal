Accessibility

Atlas Global is committed to making its website accessible, usable, and clear for as many visitors as possible.

Effective date: May 9, 2026

1. Accessibility Commitment

Atlas Global is committed to making its website accessible and usable for as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

We aim to follow recognized accessibility best practices and to improve the website over time as content, features, and technology are updated.

2. Accessibility Standard

Our target is to align the website with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at the AA level where reasonably achievable for a static marketing website.

3. Measures We Intend to Use

  • Clear page structure with semantic headings and landmarks.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Sufficient color contrast for text, buttons, links, and navigation elements.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive elements.
  • Visible focus styles for links, buttons, and form controls.
  • Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
  • Reduced-motion support for users who prefer less animation.
  • Plain-language content where practical.
  • Consistent navigation and footer links across pages.

4. Known Limitations

This website is in development. Some placeholder pages, media assets, embedded tools, third-party links, forms, or downloadable documents may not yet fully meet the intended accessibility standard.

The hero video should remain decorative, muted, and should not contain essential information unless equivalent text is provided. Any final video should avoid rapid flashing and should not rely on audio to communicate critical content.

5. Third-Party Services

Some third-party services such as scheduling tools, WhatsApp, social media platforms, analytics tools, or form processors may be outside our direct control. We will make reasonable efforts to choose vendors and tools that support accessibility.

6. Feedback and Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the website, contact us. Please include the page URL, a description of the issue, the device/browser you are using, and the assistive technology involved, if applicable.

7. Ongoing Review

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We plan to review the site periodically and address issues as they are identified, especially before public launch, after adding forms, and after adding downloadable materials.
