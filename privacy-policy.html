Privacy Policy

How Atlas Global collects, uses, shares, and protects information submitted through the website and sourcing inquiry process.

Effective date: May 9, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Atlas Global collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit our website, submit a sourcing request, contact our team, or otherwise interact with us online.

This draft is intended for website launch planning and should be reviewed by legal counsel before publication.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you, automatically through the website, and from business contacts or referral sources.

  • Contact information: name, company name, title, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, city, country, and preferred language.
  • Company information: company type, website, sourcing method, supplier region, product focus, and business needs.
  • Product and project information: product descriptions, photos, reference samples, tech packs, quantity, size range, fabric or composition, target price, timeline, packaging requirements, shipping destination, and related project notes.
  • Files you upload: tech packs, product specifications, reference photos, and other documents you submit for sourcing review.
  • Website usage information: browser type, device type, pages viewed, time on site, referring pages, IP address, general location data, and similar analytics data.
  • Communications: messages, emails, form submissions, call notes, and follow-up information.

3. How We Use Information

  • Review sourcing opportunities and determine next steps.
  • Communicate with you about products, quotes, samples, production status, logistics, and related services.
  • Coordinate qualified opportunities with Atlas Global LTD and sourcing partners.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website and client intake process.
  • Maintain internal CRM records, follow-up tasks, and commission records.
  • Send requested information, updates, or business communications.
  • Protect our rights, supplier relationships, confidential information, and business operations.
  • Comply with legal, accounting, tax, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information only as needed to operate the business and provide sourcing-related services.

  • Atlas Global LTD: to review qualified sourcing opportunities, pricing, production feasibility, samples, logistics, and transaction terms.
  • Service providers: website hosting, form processing, email, CRM, analytics, file storage, scheduling, and related business tools.
  • Professional advisors: attorneys, accountants, tax advisors, and consultants.
  • Business partners or sourcing partners: only as needed for quote review, sampling, production, logistics, or related project evaluation.
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, subpoena, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and business interests.

We do not intend to sell personal information. We do not intend to share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising without appropriate notice and consent where required.

5. Confidential Product Information

Product ideas, tech packs, designs, photos, reference samples, specifications, packaging concepts, pricing targets, and sourcing strategy may be confidential. Serious client opportunities may require a separate NNN, NDA, client engagement agreement, or transaction terms before sensitive information is exchanged or used.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand website performance, traffic sources, and user behavior. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them clearly.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No website, email system, or online transmission is completely secure. Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive information unless specifically requested and protected by appropriate agreements or secure transfer methods.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal, accounting, tax, sourcing, CRM, dispute-resolution, and recordkeeping purposes. Retention periods may vary based on the type of information, client relationship, and applicable obligations.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or limits on certain uses of your personal information. You may also request that we stop sending non-essential communications.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the contact information listed below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. International Transfers

Because Atlas Global works with Mexico-based clients and Atlas Global LTD’s Hong Kong sourcing operation, information may be processed or shared across borders. By submitting information, you understand that it may be transferred to and processed in countries with different privacy laws than your country of residence.

11. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for business users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. Third-Party Links

The website may link to third-party platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, scheduling tools, or file-sharing services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above indicates when it was last updated. Material changes should be reviewed before they are published.
