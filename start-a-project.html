Start with what you know.

You do not need to have every detail finalized. The more information you can provide, the easier it is to understand the opportunity and guide the conversation.

Helpful information to include

  • Product type or category
  • Photos, samples, or references
  • Quantity range
  • Fabric or composition, if known
  • Target price, if available
  • Desired timeline
  • Shipping destination
