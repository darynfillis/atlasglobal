Why fabric sourcing matters

Fabric can determine the look, feel, performance, cost, and quality of a garment. The right fabric decision can shape the entire product outcome.

Who may need fabric sourcing

Manufacturers, apparel brands, uniform companies, and textile importers may need fabric sourcing support when local options are limited or when they want to explore Asia-side material options.

What information helps

Useful starting details include fabric type, composition, weight, color, stretch, finish, end use, target price, quantity, and destination.

Finished garment vs. fabric-only sourcing

Some buyers need completed apparel. Others need fabric or materials to support production elsewhere. Atlas Global can help review both types of opportunities.

How to start

Send the fabric details you know, along with photos, swatches, reference links, or the intended garment use.
