Production + Specialty Capabilities

From core apparel programs to specialty finishing techniques.

Atlas Global’s Asia-side manufacturing network supports a wide range of apparel programs, including babies’ and kids’ apparel, men’s and women’s apparel, basics, polos, specialty printing, garment dye, embroidery, finishing, quality assurance, and delivery coordination.

01

Category Production

Manufacturing support for babies’ and kids’ apparel, men’s and women’s apparel, basics, t-shirts, polos, and related apparel categories.

02

Specialty Printing

Production capabilities may include foil, flock, puffer, all-over print, hot-fix, glitter, and other specialty print techniques depending on product needs.

03

Garment + Yarn Dye

Support for dye-focused apparel programs, including garment-dye and yarn-dye techniques where applicable.

04

Embroidery + Embossing

Support for apparel details such as embroidery, embossed techniques, and other specialty surface applications.

05

Quality Assurance

Counter-sample development and production review focused on matching client direction for quality, sizing, weight, and finishing.

06

Certified Production Partners

Access to production partners with certifications such as SMETA, GOTS, and F.A.M.A., depending on the product category, production path, and client requirements.
