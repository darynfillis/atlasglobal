Step by Step

Simple enough to start. Structured enough to produce seriously.

The process is designed to help your team share the right information, understand the opportunity, and move forward without unnecessary confusion.

Review before production moves forward Product details, samples, sizing, weight, and finish expectations are reviewed so the manufacturing path is clearer before production begins.
01

Share Your Product Concept

Start with the basics: product category, photos or references, quantity, size range, target price if available, timeline, and shipping destination.

Submit product details
02

Review the Opportunity

The Atlas Global Mexico team reviews the information, asks any useful follow-up questions, and helps clarify what is needed to move forward.

03

Coordinate the Manufacturing Path

Once the project direction is clear, the team coordinates the sourcing conversation with Atlas Global LTD’s Hong Kong operation.

04

Review Quote and Sample Direction

Your team receives a clearer view of pricing direction, sample path, timing, and the information needed to evaluate the opportunity.

05

Move Toward Production and Delivery

After the product direction is aligned, Atlas Global helps support the move toward sample review, production planning, and delivery coordination.

What Makes It Work

Better inputs create better sourcing conversations.

You do not need a perfect package to start. But clear information helps the team respond faster and with better direction.

Visual References

Photos, samples, sketches, website links, or similar product references help define what you want to create or source.

Commercial Direction

Quantity, target price, timeline, and size range help determine whether the opportunity is ready for sourcing review.

Decision-Maker Clarity

Knowing who will review pricing, samples, and timelines helps the process stay focused.
